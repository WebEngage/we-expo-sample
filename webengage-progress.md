## How to undo
- **Setup (Init):** delete `app.config.js`; delete `src/analytics/WebEngageManager.ts` and its import and `init()` call in `src/app/_layout.tsx`; remove the `.env.example` file and the `.env` line in `.gitignore`; run `npm uninstall react-native-webengage webengage-expo webengage-expo-push`. `ios/` is regenerated by the next prebuild, so nothing else is needed. Rebuild afterwards.
- **Push (iOS):** the push entries are inside `app.config.js` (deleted above); delete `nse/NotificationService.merged.swift` and `plugins/withWebEngageNseSource.js`; remove `"podfileTarget": true` from your `withDemoPushExtensions` entry in `app.json`. Re-run prebuild, or regenerate `ios/`.
- Data already sent to WebEngage stays there; removing it is done in the dashboard or through support@webengage.com.
